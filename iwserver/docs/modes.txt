Maps and Modes

Choose Modes, then Free for All, in the lobby. All four maps use Free for All rules; these are different places to explore, not separate unfinished game modes.

Shattersea: Ferry quays, a tidal race with two crossings, shipwright and rope loft, clinic, home and lighthouse headland.
Coruscant: Freight streets and workshops below connected civic skywalks, with a clinic, bank, food hall, archive and apartment.
Ghost Town: Saloon, assay bank, sheriff's cells, doctor, forge, railway depot, dry wash, orchard and cemetery chapel.
Freya's Ascent: Mill village and rescue station below ravine trails, an observatory shelf and summit sanctuary.

Each map declares twelve distributed spawn locations checked by the server. Floors, furniture and headroom are checked before placement. The existing short, silent spawn grace period still applies.

Furniture is destructible cover, not an inventory item or a source of endless loot. Different materials resist bullets and blasts differently. Destroyed furniture remains absent until the map is reloaded or the server restarts. Service fixtures are separate and retain their own stock, costs, cooldowns and restoration rules.
