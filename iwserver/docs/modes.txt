Maps and Modes

In the lobby, the four maps are at the top of the list: choose one and press Enter to deploy. Every map uses Free for All rules.

Ghost Town: an abandoned modern city on a six-by-six street grid, with a river, a viaduct, a cathedral, a foundry and a hospital.
Shattersea: nine islands in a tidal sea joined by twelve bridges, with the old city under water.
Freya's Ascent: a city climbing a mountain on six terraces, with an orbital ring above it.
Coruscant: six levels of the Galactic City, from Level 1313 up to the Senate and the Jedi Temple.

Each map has at least sixteen spawn points, all checked for floor, headroom and a route to the rest of the map.

Furniture is destructible cover, not loot. Different materials resist bullets and blasts differently. Destroyed furniture stays gone until the map is reloaded or the server restarts. Services such as vending machines and cash machines keep their own stock, prices and cooldowns.
