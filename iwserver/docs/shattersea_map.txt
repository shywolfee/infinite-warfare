Shattersea Map

Shattersea is a compact coastal settlement split by a tidal race, with a new layout throughout. The old harbour has a ferry waiting hall, ticket office and luggage inspection room. Netmakers Row leads to the shipwright's fitting floor, sail repair store and upstairs rope loft. A salvage yard occupies the eastern sand spit.

Ropewalk's timber crossing and the stone Customs causeway connect the harbour to Lantern island. Lantern Lane leads past a multi-room clinic and a netmaker's home with an upper sleeping room. The headland stair reaches the lantern keeper's signal room, battery workshop and sea-wall lookout.

Buildings have separate rooms, named doorways and physical alternate routes. Furniture provides destructible cover and real surfaces. ATMs, vending, medical stabilization and workshop services are placed at specific usable locations. Twelve distributed safe spawn points replace a single central arrival. The map covers coordinates 0 through 420 on both horizontal axes. There is no global ocean loop.
