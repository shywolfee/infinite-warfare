World editor

The world editor is how maps and game content are made and changed from
inside the game. Anyone with building permission (developers, administrators
and the builder staff role) opens it with the builder key, Alt+Shift+P by
default; the key can be rebound like any other. Every change is checked by
the server before it is written, backed up, applied at once for everyone on
the map, and can be undone and redone.

The editor's main list

What is here
  Everything defined at the spot you are standing on -- surfaces, zones,
  acoustic spaces, ambience, openings, landmarks, spawn points, fixtures --
  newest first, because where definitions overlap the newest one wins.
  Enter on any of them to edit it.

Build something
  Builders that make a whole thing at once, with every zone, acoustic space
  and ambience it needs, named from what you give them:
    Street: carriageway, both pavements and kerbs, each its own zone
      ("Mill Lane, east pavement"), with a street acoustic and ambience.
    Building: any number of floors, walls, floors and roof of your choosing,
      stairs between floors (or a ladder where there is no room for them),
      a door on the side facing you, windows on every floor, a zone and
      acoustic space for each floor ("Harbour Flats, second floor"), the
      roof, the entrance, and optionally a landmark.
    District: a grid of streets and junctions ("Market Street and Park
      Road") with a building on every block, in a residential, commercial
      or industrial style.
    Square, park, car park, open ground: a surface with its name, or its
      corners and middle named separately, acoustics and ambience.
    Water: a river, pond or harbour basin with its ambience.
    Stairs and ramps up or down any number of levels; ladders; bridges and
      raised walkways with railings, piers and stairs down at each end;
      walls; fences; doorways cut through whatever wall is ahead of you;
      clearing a space; naming an area; spawn points.
  You place things in plain terms -- starting just ahead of you, centred on
  you, to your left or right, behind you, or in the area you marked -- and
  choose which way they run. The form shows what will be built, where, and
  how much of the area is already built on, and updates as you change any
  setting. Nothing is written until you press Build.

Describe what you want
  Type it: "a three storey brick building 20 by 12 called Harbour Flats to
  my left", "a 60 long street called Mill Lane going north", "an industrial
  district 150 by 150 ahead", "a pond 30 by 20 called Duck Pond here". The
  editor picks the builder, fills in what you said -- name, size, floors,
  materials, where, which way -- and opens the builder with it, so you can
  check and adjust before building.

Mark an area
  Stand on one corner and mark it, then the opposite corner. You can then
  list or delete everything in it, cover it with a surface, clear it, name
  it, or build into it.

Browse and edit everything on this map
  Every element by kind, nearest first, or a search by name. Opening one
  lets you edit its fields by name (west edge, top, surface, name...), move
  it, copy it where you stand or beside it, go to it, read its raw line, or
  delete it.

This map
  Its title; whether it is listed in the lobby, and the description the
  lobby reads out; its size; the footstep sound for each surface; statistics;
  validation; undo and redo; the history of changes; reloading it from its
  file.

Every map
  Every map on the server, with its size and who is on it. Go to any of them
  -- you are moved straight there, without the lobby -- or make a new one:
  give it an id, title, size and ground, and it is created, loaded and
  optionally you are taken to it. New maps start unlisted, so only builders
  see them in the lobby until you list them.

Content
  Item and weapon files. Open one to see its settings as a list; change,
  add or remove settings, edit the whole file as text, move it to another
  folder, copy it as a new item or weapon, or delete it (the server keeps a
  backup). Saving writes the file and reloads it on the server. Items are
  sent to every connected player straight away, so what the game says an item
  does changes for everyone at once. Weapon changes apply on the server
  immediately and on each player's own game after a restart. You can also
  reload items, weapons, maps or everything from their files, after editing
  them outside the game, without restarting the server.

Go somewhere
  To coordinates, a point of interest, a zone by name, another player (on
  any map), a spawn point, or another map.

Also: add a fixture, a destructible object, or a room, open region, opening
or landmark area where you stand; and undo and redo from the main list.

Notes
  The editor's surfaces, acoustic kinds and ambience beds are listed in
  content/editor (surfaces.txt, acoustics.txt, ambience.txt), the same
  palette the map generator in tools/mapgen uses.
  Server-side physics maps (the optional native physics build) are compiled
  from map files with tools/compile_physics_maps.py and are not updated by
  the editor.
  Every change is backed up to administration/map_backups, and content
  deletions to administration/content_backups.
