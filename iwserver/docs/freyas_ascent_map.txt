Freya's Ascent Map

Freya's Ascent is a mountain settlement rising through Freya Valley. Four broad
terraces climb from the Lower Settlement through Pinewatch Terrace and
Shieldmaiden Terrace to Freya Citadel. Long contiguous ramps connect every
height, so the ascent is physical terrain rather than a teleport or staircase.

Each terrace has an individually enclosed lodge, storehouse and guard hall with
its own materials, navigation and acoustics. Beneath Shieldmaiden Terrace, a
descending stone passage leads into the dripping Rootvault Caverns.

You deploy in the Lower Settlement. Follow Freya Road or the central ascent to
reach the upper terraces. The canonical map source is
tools/rebuild_world_maps.py, and its generated map should not be edited directly.
