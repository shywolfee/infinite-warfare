Coruscant Map

Coruscant is a vertical city of three independent decks above the lightless
undercity: the industrial Works Level at Z 60, the Civic Level at Z 220 and the
Sky Level at Z 380. Aurora Spine, Republic Axis and Centax Way cross Senate
Traverse, Monument Row and Orbital Road on every level.

Each deck has four distinct enclosed interiors and a raised transit forum with
walkable ramps. The Works contains the Droid Foundry, Transit Machine Hall,
Power Regulation Vault and Underdeck Clinic. The Civic deck contains the
Republic Archive, Judicial Hall, Galactic Exchange and Civic Security Bureau.
The Sky deck contains Orbital Customs, Diplomatic Residence, Skyline
Observatory and Executive Landing Control.

The Republic Axis grand turbolift connects all three decks. Stand on its control
area and press Shift+P to choose a level. You deploy on the Civic Level. The
canonical map source is tools/rebuild_world_maps.py.
