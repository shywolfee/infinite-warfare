# Companion command center and direct control

Deploy an Automatic Hover Drone, Bomber Hover Drone or Bioengineered Chimp
Scout Beacon from your inventory. Each item creates a separate actor with
its own health, location, heading, attack and sounds. You can have eight out
at once. Open **Control deployed companions** in the dashboard, or use a
Companion Command Remote.

## Live command center

Select an actor in **Deployed companions**, then Tab to its live telemetry
and orders. Health, coordinates, map, heading and orders update without
closing the screen. Execute an order for the selected actor or your entire
fleet. Direct piloting always controls just one actor.

| Order | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Follow | Travel with your body and defend against nearby targets |
| Stay | Hold position without automatically attacking |
| Guard | Defend the selected position and return there after combat |
| Seek and attack | Pursue visible hostile targets within acquisition range |
| Use attack once | Attack an unobstructed hostile within weapon range |
| Recall | Travel back to your body before returning the item to inventory |
| Pilot directly | Switch movement and perception to this actor |

Attacks have an individual recharge time. Walls block targeting; team-mates
and arena spectators are not targets. Acquisition range is not weapon range:
an autonomous actor may need to approach before attacking. Piloted attacks
select targets in front of the actor, so turn toward the enemy first.

## Piloting

Your body stays where it really is and remains vulnerable. Sounds, heading,
zone reads, coordinates and the visual camera now come from the companion.
Your inventory and equipped weapons stay with your body.

- Movement keys move the companion; Q and E turn it by default.
- Page Up and Page Down raise or lower drones. Chimps stay on the ground.
- The fire key uses the companion's attack, respecting its recharge time.
- H or F reads telemetry, C reads coordinates and B reads its current zone.
- Escape returns to your body. You can also choose Return to your body from
  the companion dashboard or command center.
- O opens the command center (the configured vehicle-door interaction key).
- Tab works with your selected dashboard/inventory mode. The piloting
  dashboard offers telemetry, attacks, commands and communication; ordinary
  body-use actions are unavailable until you return.
  In inventory mode, Tab opens companion control instead of cycling the
  body's inventory while piloting.

Movement, turning, altitude and fire reuse your configured keys. On touch,
the existing movement drags control the actor, rotation gestures turn it,
aim gestures change drone altitude, the fire gesture attacks, and status or
facing reads telemetry. The context-actions gesture opens companion control;
the game-menu gesture returns to your body. Dashboard gestures still work.

Recall companions before leaving a map. Map transfers, death, disconnects
and destruction remove deployed actors and end piloting safely. Returning
from piloting alone leaves the companion in Stay mode, ready for new orders.
