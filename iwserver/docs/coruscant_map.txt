Coruscant

A vertical slice of the Galactic City through the Senate District, down to the undercity. Four hundred units across, six levels deep.

Level 1313, at the bottom: the Crimson Corridor, the sump market, the Blackpipe clinic and the drop shop. The Works: a dead foundry with a crane rail over its casting floor, the coolant court and the pumping gallery. CoCo Town: Coruscant Avenue, Dex's Diner, the Collective Commerce machine shops and the medcentre. The Uscru Entertainment District: Vos Gesal Street, the Outlander Club and the Galaxies Opera House, with a stair to the boxes. The Senate District, on top: the Senate Rotunda, the Republic Executive Building, and Monument Plaza with Umate, the one piece of the original planet you can stand on. Across the skylane is the Jedi Temple on its four-setback ziggurat, with the Room of a Thousand Fountains and the processional hall on the top and a ladder up the tallest of its five spires. Above everything are three skylane gantries and the interchange that joins them, reached by rungs from the esplanade.

Every level is a street grid with buildings between the streets. Three public turbolifts -- western, central and eastern -- stand in the same place on every level and serve all five. Two vent shafts of maintenance rungs run from Level 1313 up through the Works; they are the reason the undercity is not sealed.
