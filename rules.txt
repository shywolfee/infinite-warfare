# Infinite Warfare server rules

These rules apply to public chat, private messages and gameplay.

1. **No insults.** You will be asked to stop. Continued insults lead to a kick, and continuing after that leads to a ban.
2. **No chat spam.** Spamming leads to a kick. Returning and continuing to spam leads to a ban.
3. **No cheating.** If a player or administrator notices cheating, you will be asked to stop. Continuing leads to a ban.
4. **No impersonation.** Do not pretend to be another player or use their nickname. Impersonation leads to a ban.
5. **Follow administrator instructions.** If an administrator asks you to stop doing something, stop.
6. **Do not spam feedback submissions.** Abusing the feedback command leads to a ban.

Check these rules periodically in case they change.
