Getting Started

After deployment you receive a random melee weapon, sidearm and primary weapon, plus basic healing and explosives. Automatic starting quickbar assignment can be enabled or disabled in Gameplay options.

Use the arrow keys to move and hold Shift with movement to sprint. Control fires, R reloads, Shift+R unloads, A reports ammunition, Shift+A changes fire mode, Alt+R selects a compatible ammunition load for the next reload, and Alt+A opens the drawn weapon panel.

Press I for the categorized inventory. Tab and Shift+Tab still cycle items directly; K and Shift+K cycle inventory categories. Number keys activate quickbar slots and Alt+number assigns the selected item.

Press Shift+H for the help browser. Its weapon, ammunition, attachment, armour and item sections are generated from the live content database. Alt+Enter opens database help for the selected inventory entry.
