Controls

All configurable actions can be changed in Options, Controls, Key binding editor and stored in separate keymap profiles. Shift+F1 opens key practice.

Touch access: four-finger double tap opens a dynamic list containing only the gestures relevant to the current audio form, menu, map, vehicle, or touch keyboard. Options, Display can retain the system keyboard or enable Infinite Warfare's touch keyboard. Explore-by-touch typing uses a simulated QWERTY surface: drag to hear a key and lift to type it. Analogue typing navigates the keyboard grid with one-finger directional swipes and types with a double tap; two-finger directional swipes jump to the requested middle edge position. Both layouts include Shift, Caps Lock, Backspace, punctuation, Space, and Done.

Middle-band movement arbitration: holding and dragging one finger claims that contact for continuous movement and suppresses its pending aim swipe. A quick one-finger directional swipe still performs the bound aim action when released. This prevents a movement drag from activating aim up or down at the same time.

Touch-screen laptop access: Options, Display can expand the three touch bands over the complete physical display by enabling touch mode and true full screen together. Menu explore by touch is on by default and can be disabled there. To explore, hold one finger nearly still for 450 milliseconds and then drag; controls under its vertical position are announced. Moving before the hold threshold cancels exploration, so ordinary swipes, taps, and double taps are unaffected. In audio forms, two-finger swipe right acts as Tab and two-finger swipe left as Shift+Tab, allowing entry into and exit from options category pages.

Movement: Arrow keys. Hold Shift while moving to sprint. Space jumps. Alt+Z sits or stands. Page Up and Page Down move vertically where supported.
Aim: Q and E turn; Shift+Q and Shift+E fine-turn. S and X change vertical aim; Shift provides fine adjustment. Alt+Left Bracket and Alt+Right Bracket select a target region; Alt+T resets to the whole body.
Combat: Control fires. R reloads conventional weapons. Shift+R unloads conventional weapons. Alt+N operates the drawn weapon's class-specific control, Alt+Shift+N services or vents it, and Alt+Shift+A inspects its handling state. Revolvers and shotguns are operated manually: Alt+Y opens or closes the cylinder/action, Alt+I inserts one selected round, Alt+Shift+Y cycles a closed shotgun action, and Alt+Shift+G ejects rounds while open. A reports ammunition and manual-action state. Shift+A changes fire mode. Alt+R selects the next compatible ammunition load. Alt+A opens the drawn weapon panel.
Inventory: I opens the categorized inventory. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle items. K and Shift+K cycle categories. Enter uses the selected item, Shift+Enter opens its weapon panel when applicable, and Alt+Enter opens dynamic item help. Alt+Shift+M cycles the selected item's operating configuration or exact medication dose; Alt+Shift+K reports its complete mechanics, recommendation and current configuration. Alt+Shift+P removes one transferable pill from the selected medication bottle. Alt+number binds a quickbar slot.
Information: H checks health, V checks stamina and effects, Alt+B reads buffs and debuffs, F reports facing, C reports coordinates, B reports the zone, M reports nearby content, P reports nearby players, and J lists points of interest.
Communication: Slash opens global chat. Backslash opens current-map chat. Shift+Backslash opens team chat. Alt+Backslash opens recent private-message contacts. Alt+O controls voice transmission, Alt+V configures the current voice room, and Shift+F7 opens voice settings.
Server information: F1 players, F2 message of the day, F3 ping statistics, F4 uptime and traffic.
Vehicles: O controls the nearby door, V automatically enters the nearest open vehicle and sits in an available seat, U opens or closes your window, Alt+Left/Right changes seats, arrows control motion and turning, and Space handles vehicle-specific actions where available. Vehicles occupy their full physical footprint. You may instead open a door, walk into the interior, approach a physical seat and press V to sit there; press V again to stand at that seat inside the cabin. Walking around a vehicle announces its front, rear, sides, cabin and roof zones.
Menus and display: Escape opens the game menu, F11 opens Options, Ctrl+Shift+F12 toggles true full screen, Shift+H opens Help, Alt+E opens Equipment, Alt+S opens Support, and Shift+I opens the quickbar and equipment-ability manager. Full screen can also be persisted from the Display category in Options; it fills the touch surface so an edge tap cannot activate a neighboring desktop window. Equipment abilities are automatically assigned to an available quickbar slot and can be moved there.
