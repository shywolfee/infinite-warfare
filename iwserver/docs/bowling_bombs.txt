# Momentum bowling bombs

Press Enter to throw: bowling bombs do not have a pin or a cookable fuse.
A near-level throw gives the longest path, roughly 120 tiles. Steeper throws
reduce the budget toward 90 tiles. The budget measures the path travelled,
including direction changes, not the distance from the thrower.

## Rolling, ramps and ledges

The bomb gains speed on level ground. Ramp gravity slows uphill throws and
then pulls them back downhill. Downhill travel builds momentum. If it comes
back toward you, move aside: it can hit its owner and explode, or pass beside
you and continue rolling in the opposite direction.

Leaving a ledge sends it into a downward arc. Hitting the ground plays its
landing sound and removes some momentum. It then resumes accelerating.
The rolling sound follows the bomb throughout its journey.

## Detonation

Hitting a player, companion, object, vehicle or wall detonates the bomb.
Landing on the ground does not. Exhausting its path budget detonates it too.
Greater travel distance and speed increase blast and fragment damage; cover
and the game's normal damage rules still apply. An abandoned, trapped bomb
also has a two-minute safety detonation rather than remaining forever.

Its range, acceleration, speed ceiling, landing retention and damage bonuses
are editable properties of the bowling bomb in the content editor.
