Commands

Press / (slash) to open the chat input. Type a message and press Enter to send it. Messages starting with / are treated as commands.

Player commands:
/bunkers - Show any bunker locations on your current map.
/team info - Show your team roster, roles, and shared point pool.
/team shop - Show the team shop and prices. Full management is available from the in-game Team menu.
/pm [name] [message] - Send a private message to another player.
/bug - Submit a bug report with a title and description.
/bugs - View your bug reports and their status.
/feedback [message] - Submit feedback to the developers.
/changes - View the changelog.

Staff commands, subject to the account's server role:
/accounts - Browse server accounts and moderation status.
/warn [account] [reason], /warnings [account] - Record or review warnings.
/ban [account] [reason], /unban [account], /bans - Manage account bans.
/staff - List persistent staff assignments.
/staffrole [account] [administrator|moderator|support|builder] - Developer-only role assignment.
/unstaff [account] - Developer-only role removal.

You can also open the command menu with Alt+C to run any command without typing it manually.
