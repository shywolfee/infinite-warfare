Vehicles

Vehicles are physical world objects with type-specific health, armour, passenger capacity, acceleration, braking and maximum speed. Players can collide with them, climb or sit on their roofs, be carried at safe speeds, and be thrown off by excessive acceleration or aircraft launch.

Controls:
  Press O within 5 tiles to open or close the vehicle door.
  Press V to enter or exit the vehicle.
  UP/DOWN arrows apply acceleration or braking through the vehicle's continuous speed model.
  LEFT/RIGHT arrows turn 90 degrees. Shift+LEFT/RIGHT turns 1 degree.
  Alt+LEFT/RIGHT switches between driver and passenger seats. Only the driver controls movement.
  Press U to roll the window down or up at your seat. The action can be rebound as Vehicle Window.

You can only fire weapons with the window down. Melee weapons cannot be used from vehicles. Press H in a vehicle to see vehicle health, armor, speed level, seat, and window status.

Stamina restores while seated. Vehicles take damage from projectile impacts and collisions, including shots fired by their owner. Armour mitigates damage until depleted. When destroyed, occupants are ejected and injured. Every vehicle type has independent door, window, seat, engine, motion, impact and destruction audio where available.
