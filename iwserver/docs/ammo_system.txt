Ammunition System

Every registered weapon declares its calibre, feed device, capacity and standard reserve in the content database. Each weapon takes exactly one ammunition: the ordinary load of its own calibre. There are no alternate loads to choose between.

Press A for the loaded calibre, quantity and remaining reserve; press R to reload and Shift+R to unload. Alt+A opens the live weapon panel.

Magazine and cell swaps preserve the removed feed device as a partial. Revolvers and individually fed shotguns are genuinely manual: Alt+Y opens or closes the cylinder or action, Alt+I inserts exactly one round, Alt+Shift+G ejects the contents while open, and Alt+Shift+Y cycles a closed tube-fed pump action. Break actions chamber when closed, while SGX detachable-drum shotguns use magazine swaps. The fire control explains whether an open action, empty chamber, or missed cycle prevents firing. Extended and drum magazines change real capacity and reserve consumption. Energy weapons preserve actual remaining charge. Fuel weapons draw only the required fuel from full or partial canisters.

The Help browser's Ammo categories are generated from the current ammunition database. Each entry reports every compatible weapon, including shotgun shells, revolver ammunition, rifle calibres, mortar rounds, crossbow bolts and energy cells.

Using a reserve or partial ammunition container explains that ammunition is loaded rather than used directly and lists the compatible weapons you currently carry, including weapons in holsters. Use the drawn weapon's reload controls instead.
