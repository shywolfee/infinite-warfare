Item Mechanics and Configuration

Items use capability-specific mechanics rather than one generic effect. Select an item and press Alt+Shift+K to hear its complete dynamic mechanics. If the item supports alternate operation, Alt+Shift+M cycles its persistent configuration. Both actions are configurable in the key binding editor, and current configuration also appears in dynamic Help and Armoury entries.

Thrown explosives offer controlled-lob, standard and forceful-long-throw delivery. These change authoritative horizontal velocity, vertical launch velocity, arc, flight time, bounce opportunity and practical placement while retaining each payload's own fuse, radius, damage and status effects. Fragmentation, impact, flash, concussion, incendiary and gas payloads remain mechanically distinct.

Proximity mines, claymores and timed charges offer rapid, balanced and concealed arming. Rapid placement arms in 650 milliseconds with a smaller sensor envelope. Balanced placement arms in 1.2 seconds. Concealed placement takes 2.2 seconds but expands proximity or directional detection by thirty percent. Facing, ownership, team safety, maximum deployed count and payload behavior remain authoritative.

Generic health potions have been replaced by measured medications. Nanocyte Reconstruction Solution, Trauma Repair Serum, Hemostatic Coagulant Serum and Medigel Recovery Tonic are multi-dose liquid bottles measured in fluid ounces. Regenex, Neural Pain-Blocker, Synth-Clot, Valkyr Combat Stimulant and Broad-Spectrum Antitoxin are counted pill bottles. Each drug has its own capacity, recommendation, immediate effect, residual amount, duration and toxicity profile. Alt+Shift+M selects the exact dose and Alt+Shift+K reports the recommendation and risk. Bottles remain until their contents are exhausted.

Red Spark, Blue Surge, Green Overdrive and Rainbow Hypercharge are energy drinks rather than instant potions. Drinking takes 1.8 seconds and uses the normal health-potion drinking recording. Each colour and selected serving size contributes a different stimulant load; that load metabolizes gradually, but stacking it above the safe threshold schedules a delayed Energy Crash that drains stamina and impairs movement for twenty seconds. Conservative half-servings spaced apart are much less likely to cross the threshold.

Medication toxicity accumulates across all drugs and decays gradually. High exposure announces a warning; exceeding safe exposure inflicts immediate and continuing damage and can be fatal. Antitoxin reduces toxicity. Alt+Shift+P removes one pill from a selected bottle as a loose inventory item, allowing individual tablets or capsules to be used or transferred without giving away the complete bottle. All medication administration retains the health-potion sound.

Bags and boxes support medical, balanced and ammunition-priority packing. Their selected profile changes the supplies and ammunition awarded when the sealed container is consumed. Water containers retain a connected loop: an empty bucket must be filled while standing in water, carried to a tank and emptied over time.

C4, Semtex components and controllers retain persistent owner-tracked placement and remote detonation. Smoke, torpedoes, bunker explosives, rocks and decoys retain their individual deployment, cooldown, targeting and environmental roles. Team items require membership and invoke shared vehicle, supply, armour, revival or reconnaissance operations.

Armour protects only its equipped anatomical region, loses durability from absorbed hits and contributes physical mass. Holsters provide compatible weapon and ammunition storage. Attachments occupy real weapon slots and alter live statistics. Ammunition preserves calibre, load behavior and partial feed-device contents. Biomonitor, WireNet, HyperComp, translocator and repair equipment each retain their dedicated interfaces, charges, abilities and component requirements.

Firearm support content includes class-specific cleaning tools, patches, solvents, brushes, lubricants, thermal and dielectric supplies, replacement barrels, gunsmith tools, gauges, malfunction-clearing tools and mechanical replacement parts. This includes the flamethrower's Fuel System Wrench, which remains Weapon Maintenance despite its fuel-related name. Vehicle support content includes typed fuel, approved containers, siphoning equipment, reusable service tools, fluids, filters, tyres, cooling and electrical parts, contamination treatments and permanent refit packages. Every one of these is a first-class dynamic database item shown under Weapon Maintenance or Vehicle Supplies and included by developer item grants.

The obsolete Waypoint Marker has been removed from the content database, inventory categorization, use handling and audio-import manifest. Points of interest, tracking, translocation and map navigation already provide maintained navigation systems.
