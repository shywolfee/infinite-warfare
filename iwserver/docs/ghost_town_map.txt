Ghost Town

A modern city of about half a million people that everybody left. Four hundred and eighty units across.

The grid: six avenues run north to south -- Harbour Way, Meridian Avenue, Cathedral Street, Foundry Street, Eastgate Avenue and Orbital Road -- and six streets run east to west -- Dockside Road, Fourth Street, Market Street, Civic Parade, Northgate Road and Orbital Road North. Every street has a carriageway, two pavements and a kerb between them, and is named by the stretch you are on: "Meridian Avenue between Fourth Street and Market Street, west pavement". Every junction is its own place, with a zone for each crossing. Every block is cut through by a service alley.

Districts, south to north: Dockside (the harbour, the cold store, the harbourmaster's office); Southbank (the fish market, the bus station, the timber yard); Downtown (Meridian Plaza between three towers, the Alder Mutual banking hall, an office and a multi-storey car park); the Old Quarter (Saint Ewald's cathedral with a ladder to its bell stage, and the covered market on cobbled lanes); the Foundry district (the foundry with a gantry above its casting floor, the gasworks and the rail yard with its locomotive shed and coaling stage); Civic Parade (City Hall, the Central Library, the police station and the hospital); and Northgate (terraced streets, one house you can enter, and the corner shop), with Corvid Park and its bandstand.

The River Alder runs down the western edge. The quay is one step above the water, so walking off it is a step you hear and walking back up is the same step. The water can be crossed anywhere, loudly; the three jetties, the pontoon and two moored barges are the quiet way onto it.

The Fourth Street viaduct runs twelve above the road from the quay to Orbital Road. Reach it by the stair beside the Meridian transit ticket hall, the stair on Foundry Street, or the maintenance rungs at Orbital Road. It is the only way across the city that never touches a junction.
