Stamina System

Stamina represents your physical endurance.
Stamina degrades when using melee weapons, jumping frequently (more than 3 jumps in 25 seconds), and moving or jumping with heavy weapons (high walk time).
Stamina restores naturally every 5 seconds.
You can also restore stamina with energetic potions which come in red, blue, green, and rainbow quality tiers.
Press V to report stamina and active effects. Press Alt+B for the full buff and debuff report.
Low stamina slows your movement and attack speed.

Hold Shift with a movement key to sprint. Sprint speed scales with remaining stamina and sprinting continuously drains stamina.

Press Alt+Z to sit or stand. Sitting greatly slows movement but regenerates stamina faster. Heavy weapons cannot be used while sitting, and seated jumps are shorter, cost more stamina, and have a seven-second cooldown.
