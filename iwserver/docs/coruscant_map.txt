Coruscant Map

Coruscant is a 1280 by 1280 vertical city with four fully traversable levels:
the Undercity Foundations at Z 0, Works Level at Z 120, Civic Level at Z 300
and Sky Level at Z 500. The footprint is four times larger than before.

Every level is divided into thirty-six uniquely named precincts. Each precinct
has different ground materials and contains either a complete enclosed building
or a raised market, garden, memorial court or industrial platform reached by a
physical ramp. Across the city there are seventy-two interiors, seventy-two
raised local landmarks, twenty major roads and four Republic Axis forums.

The Undercity contains salvage, coolant and utility districts. Works Level is
filled with foundries, freight yards and machine halls. Civic Level contains
archives, courts, ministries and public exchanges. Sky Level contains orbital,
diplomatic, executive and observation precincts. Local zone announcements name
the exact precinct, street, building or landmark rather than only the deck.

The Republic Axis grand turbolift connects all four levels. Stand on its control
area and press Shift+P to choose a destination. You deploy on the raised Civic
Level forum. The canonical map source is tools/rebuild_world_maps.py.
