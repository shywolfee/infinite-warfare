Shattersea Map

Shattersea is New Aurelia, a walled island city surrounded by beach and the
Shattersea. The Crown Canal divides the island and is crossed by four raised,
walkable bridges at Tideway, Lantern Avenue, Founders Road and Breakwater Drive.
South Quay Road, Market Street, Civic Way and Northwall Road form the principal
east-west routes.

The city contains twelve complete interiors, including the Saltglass Exchange,
Mariners Clinic, Southwatch Armoury, Ash & Anchor Tavern, Northwall Apartments,
Aurelia Library, Storm Market, Harbour Warehouse, Canal Customs House,
Ferrymaster's Hall, Crown Workshops and East Lock Station. Each is a physical
room with its own floor, walls, roof, doorway, navigation zone and acoustics.
The raised Founders Memorial overlooks the southern side of the canal.

Select Shattersea from the Free for All map picker to deploy near central New
Aurelia. The canonical map source is tools/rebuild_world_maps.py; the old
Meridian generator is retired and the generated main.map should not be edited.
