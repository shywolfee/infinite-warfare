# Item Mechanics

Items use capability-specific mechanics rather than one generic effect. Select an item and press Alt+Shift+K to hear its complete dynamic mechanics, which also appear in dynamic Help and Armoury entries. Items have no alternate operating modes.

Thrown and launched explosives follow your vertical aim: aim level for an ordinary arcing throw, up to lob it high and far, or down to drop or roll it short. Grenades can be cooked by holding them before release. Each map's wind carries them in flight. Each payload keeps its own fuse, radius, damage and status effects; fragmentation, impact, flash, concussion, incendiary and gas payloads remain mechanically distinct.

Inside a vehicle, a grenade thrown with your window open leaves through the window carrying the vehicle's speed and direction. With the window closed it goes off inside the cabin, hurting everyone in it and the vehicle badly; the game asks you to press Enter again within three seconds before letting you do that. Placing a mine or charge inside a vehicle asks for the same confirmation, and the explosive then rides along with the vehicle.

Proximity mines, claymores and timed charges arm after their own authored delay and watch their own sensor area. Facing, ownership, team safety, maximum deployed count and payload behavior remain authoritative.

Generic health potions have been replaced by measured medications. Nanocyte Reconstruction Solution, Trauma Repair Serum, Hemostatic Coagulant Serum and Medigel Recovery Tonic are multi-dose liquid bottles measured in fluid ounces. Regenex, Neural Pain-Blocker, Synth-Clot, Valkyr Combat Stimulant and Broad-Spectrum Antitoxin are counted pill bottles. Each drug has its own capacity, recommendation, immediate effect, residual amount, duration and toxicity profile. Each use of a liquid takes its recommended dose, or what is left in the bottle, and each use of a pill bottle takes its recommended number of pills. Alt+Shift+K reports the recommendation and risk. Bottles remain until their contents are exhausted.

Medication toxicity accumulates across all drugs and decays gradually. High exposure announces a warning; exceeding safe exposure inflicts immediate and continuing damage and can be fatal. Antitoxin reduces toxicity. Alt+Shift+L removes one pill from a selected bottle as a loose inventory item, allowing individual tablets or capsules to be used or transferred without giving away the complete bottle. All medication administration retains the health-potion sound.

Water containers retain a connected loop: an empty bucket must be filled while standing in water, carried to a tank and emptied over time.

C4, Semtex components and controllers retain persistent owner-tracked placement and remote detonation. Smoke, torpedoes, bunker explosives, rocks and decoys retain their individual deployment, cooldown, targeting and environmental roles. Team items require membership and invoke shared vehicle, supply, armour, revival or reconnaissance operations.

Armour protects only its equipped anatomical region, loses durability from absorbed hits and contributes physical mass. Holsters provide compatible weapon and ammunition storage. Attachments occupy real weapon slots and alter live statistics. Ammunition preserves calibre and partial feed-device contents. Biomonitor, WireNet, HyperComp, translocator and repair equipment each retain their dedicated interfaces, charges, abilities and component requirements.

Vehicle support content includes typed fuel, approved containers, siphoning equipment, reusable service tools, fluids, filters, tyres, cooling and electrical parts, contamination treatments and permanent refit packages, shown under Vehicle Supplies and included by developer item grants. The repair kit restores up to 150 health to the vehicle you are in or beside. Firearms need no maintenance supplies.

The obsolete Waypoint Marker has been removed from the content database, inventory categorization, use handling and audio-import manifest. Points of interest, tracking, translocation and map navigation already provide maintained navigation systems.
