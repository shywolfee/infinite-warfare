Fire Modes

Press Shift+A to cycle the modes supported by the drawn weapon. Semi fires once per press, burst schedules a short multi-round sequence, and automatic continues while held. Scattershot launches additional independently simulated particles with wider dispersion. Focused mode concentrates the pattern and increases projectile damage, velocity and effective range at its authored charge or durability cost.

Press Alt+A, or Shift+Enter on an inventory weapon, to see the active mode and its effect in the live damage, range, dispersion, velocity and projectile-count report. Modes not registered for that weapon are never offered.
