Game Modes

Every map in Infinite Warfare is played as a Free for All. In the lobby, open the Modes tab and choose Free for All, then pick which map you want from the map selection menu.

Available maps:

Shattersea: New Aurelia, a walled island city crossed by the Crown Canal, four raised bridges, twelve complete interiors and the elevated Founders Memorial.

Coruscant: Three independent vertical city decks—the Works, Civic and Sky levels—with distinct interiors, raised transit forums and a shared grand turbolift. Press Shift+P on its control area to travel.

Ghost Town: A sprawling, deserted city. The largest open street map, formerly known as Free for All.

Freya's Ascent: A mountain settlement of four rising terraces, twelve enclosed buildings, long walkable ramps and the subterranean Rootvault Caverns.

Select a map and you will be deployed directly to it with a fresh loadout.
