Buffs and Debuffs

Status effects are timed gameplay states rather than inventory documentation. Alt+B reads every active buff and debuff as text. Health and stamina reports also include relevant current state.

Effects include residual healing, stamina and movement boosts, bleeding, burning, suppression, impaired legs, weapon sway, cryogenic slowing, shock disruption and other combat conditions. Their strength and duration come from the item, ammunition or hit that applied them; use Alt+Enter on that content entry for its current database description.
