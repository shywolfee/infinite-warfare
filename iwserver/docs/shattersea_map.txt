Shattersea Map

Shattersea is Greater New Aurelia, a 970 by 970 island city surrounded by a
quiet beach and sea. The Crown Canal divides four large wards and is crossed by
five raised, walkable bridges at Tideway, Lantern Avenue, Founders Road, Crown
Boulevard and Breakwater Drive. Six east-west routes complete the city grid.

The Southwatch, Eastwater, Lantern and Northwall wards contain twenty-eight
complete interiors: civic buildings, homes, shops, industrial works, medical
facilities, guard houses, entertainment venues, ferry terminals and canal
stations. Each has its own floor, walls, roof, doorway, navigation and
acoustics. The raised Founders Memorial and Beacon Court provide two additional
landmarks reached by physical ramps.

Select Shattersea from the Free for All map picker to deploy near central New
Aurelia. The canonical map source is tools/rebuild_world_maps.py, and the
generated main.map should not be edited directly.
