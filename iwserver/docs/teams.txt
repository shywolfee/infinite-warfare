Teams

Open Team from the in-game menu to create or join a persistent team. Available actions are generated from your current membership, role and delegated permissions.

Owners can invite and remove members, appoint managers, delegate invitation, removal and shop control, and manage the shared point pool. Managers and members see only actions they are authorized to use. Kills contribute points to the common pool, which authorized members can spend in the Team Shop.

Shift+Backslash opens team text chat. The team also has its own live voice room. Team-specific purchasable content and its current behavior appear dynamically under Team in the inventory and Help browser.
