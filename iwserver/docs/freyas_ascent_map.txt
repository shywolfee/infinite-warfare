Freya's Ascent

A city built up the side of a mountain called Freya's Tears, on six terraces cut one above the other, with an orbital ring above it. Three hundred units across and a hundred and fifty high.

Going up: the Roots at the foot (the landing field, the freight hall and the Last Warm Room); Lowreach at fourteen (cut-rock housing and the catchment basin); the Stitchworks at thirty (the ropeworks, with a catwalk over its spinning floor, and the foundry); Middlehold at forty-eight (the market square, the hostel and the infirmary); the Cataract Terrace at sixty-six (the mill the waterfall drives, and a gantry out over the drop); Highreach at eighty-six (administration and the observatory); and the Crown at a hundred and ten, where the tether is anchored.

Each terrace is set back from the one below, so what is in front of it is the lower terrace's shelf. There are four ways up: the switchback stairs, which zigzag so no single flight is the whole climb; the goods lift, five stops and fast; the goods adits, the tunnels joining the lift to each shelf -- the lowest runs a hundred and forty units through solid rock; and the maintenance rungs straight up the western cliff face.

The waterfall lands on a different shelf at every level, always to your east and a little quieter each time you climb past it. It tells you how high you are without your asking.

Folkvangr Spindle, the orbital ring, is reached only by the tether climber on the Crown: a ring of corridor with four spokes to a hub and four compartments off it -- the wardroom, hydroponics, the sick bay and the machine deck.
