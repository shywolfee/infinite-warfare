Inventory System

Press I to open the inventory. Up and Down browse the current group, Left and Right change group, Enter selects, Shift+Enter uses the focused entry, and Alt+Enter opens its database documentation.

The live groups are All, Weapons, Ammo, Explosives, Healing, Utility, Armour, Attachments, Team and Equipment. Content is classified by the same runtime database used by the armoury and developer grant tools, so newly registered content does not require another hardcoded help list.

Tab and Shift+Tab cycle items without opening the form. K and Shift+K cycle categories. Alt plus a number assigns the current entry to that quickbar slot; the number alone activates it.

Partial magazines, cells and other feed devices remain typed inventory objects and report their remaining capacity and ammunition load. On death, carried inventory is transferred to the corpse loot system.
