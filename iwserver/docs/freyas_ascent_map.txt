Freya's Ascent Map

Freya's Ascent is a large mountain settlement spanning Greater Freya Valley.
Five broad terraces climb from the Lower Settlement through Pinewatch,
Shieldmaiden and Valkyrie terraces to Freya Citadel. A grand central ascent and
an eastern mountain trail provide two physical ramp routes between every tier.

Each terrace has five individually enclosed buildings: a lodge, craft hall,
assembly hall, provision house and guard house. Beneath the middle ascent, the
Rootvault has five large chambers connected by three passages, a descending
gallery and a dedicated cave entrance.

You deploy in the Lower Settlement. Follow Freya Road or the central ascent to
reach the upper terraces. The canonical map source is
tools/rebuild_world_maps.py, and its generated map should not be edited directly.
