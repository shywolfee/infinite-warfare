Freya's Ascent Map

The new ascent starts on a meltwater foreland below Birch village. The communal kitchen connects to expedition stores. The watermill has a wheel hall, counting room and sacks store. The rescue station has separate reception and treatment rooms.

The western ravine has an aqueduct inspection shelter and a raised aqueduct deck, with a passage beneath its arch. Western pack and eastern scree trails reach the observatory shelf at elevation 16. Its coat room, chart library and instrument workshop form a through-building route, with a separate weather battery shed.

The pilgrimage stair and exposed emergency ascent lead to the summit ledge at elevation 30. The sanctuary has a vestibule, memorial chamber and overlook. Routes use physical one-unit slopes/stairs, not teleport transitions. Furniture is destructible. Twelve authored spawn points are spread among the safe routes and elevations. Horizontal bounds are 360 by 460.
