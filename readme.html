1. Starting and navigating

The main menu provides connection and registration, account management, options, update checking, the changelog, game-sound learning, Android gesture help and practice, keyboard practice, this README, administration where permitted, source-only release packaging, and exit.

After connecting, the lobby remains live while forms are open. You can chat, inspect players and accounts, browse killstreak rewards, open the dynamic armoury, or choose Modes → Free for All and select a map. Deployment gives a random melee weapon, sidearm, primary weapon, ammunition, maintenance supplies appropriate to the starting weapons, healing equipment, and explosives.

Press Shift+H for the in-game help browser, Shift+F1 for keyboard practice, or use the Information section of the main menu. The in-game database is the authority for individual weapon, ammunition, attachment, armour, vehicle, and item statistics.

4. Combat simulation

Combat is server-authoritative and projectile based. Firearms have flight time, velocity, drag, gravity, dispersion, recoil, penetration, and weapon-specific ballistics. Shotguns launch independent pellets; energy weapons use cells, heat, and focused or scatter behaviors; bows, crossbows, mortars, and thrown explosives retain their own trajectories and flight audio.

Players are not one-point targets. Continuous, facing-aware physical models contain skull, neck, torso, pelvis, arms, and legs. A projectile intersects an actual volume, which determines damage region and the armour slot that can absorb it. Movement still uses predictable whole-coordinate navigation while the body moves continuously between accepted coordinates.

Weapon handling

  • Pistols have safeties; SMGs have stocks; assault rifles have gas settings; battle rifles can brace; precision rifles can steady and may require manual bolt cycling.
  • Machine guns support automatic fire, bipods, recoil, barrel heat, and sustained-fire consequences.
  • Energy weapons track emitter heat and thermal lockout. Fuel weapons, mortars, powered tools, crossbows, ballistae, and melee classes each expose distinct operating mechanics.
  • Revolvers and shotguns are genuinely manual-fed. Firing is refused with a contextual explanation when the cylinder, action, chamber, or cycling state is wrong.
  • Heat, fouling, alignment, and condition are shown only for weapons to which they apply. Maintenance consumes compatible supplies, takes time, has cooldowns, and can damage a weapon when performed incorrectly.

Ammunition and attachments

Weapons accept a dynamic set of real ammunition items: firearm cartridges and shells, specialist shotgun loads, bolt types, and energy cells. Ammunition changes actual projectile behavior. Attachments occupy logical slots and affect capacity, feed compatibility, velocity, recoil, dispersion, drag, range, movement, reload timing, and sound. Extended magazines alter real capacity. The weapon panel's damage, range, projectile count, dispersion, velocity, fire mode, ammunition, attachment, and condition readouts are live.

5. Inventory, equipment, and items

The content database is shared by inventory, dynamic help, the armoury, developer grants, starting kits, and “give all.” Individual content is not duplicated in this manual because it changes with the database.

Inventory categories include Weapons, Ammo, Explosives, Healing, Armour, Armour Repair, Weapon Maintenance, Vehicle Supplies, Attachments, Holsters, Team, Tools, Electronics, Containers, and Equipment. A category participates in navigation only while it contains an item.

Equipment has dedicated holster, utility, medical, bracelet, head, torso, arm, and leg slots. One armour piece occupies each applicable location. Only armour covering the body part actually struck mitigates that hit. Durability, weight, and movement penalties are authoritative. Compatible patches restore a limited amount without rebuilding destroyed armour.

Equipment such as the Biomonitor Bracelet and WireNet exposes a quickbar ability instead of consuming a hardcoded key. The Shift+I manager shows the ability, source equipment, description, cooldown or charges, and allows slot movement.

Healing medications use measured liquid quantities or pill counts, recommended doses, duration, toxicity, and overdose rules. Explosives, deployables, electronics, containers, tools, maintenance supplies, armour patches, and vehicle supplies retain their own configurations, timers, requirements, and failure cases.

6. Vehicles

Vehicles are full physical map objects with oriented dimensions, body and wheel hit volumes, acceleration, braking, momentum, fuel, health, armour, and type-specific interiors. Their floors replace underlying terrain, cabin walls and roofs move with the vehicle, and cabin footsteps and acoustics behave like a moving room.

You may open a door and physically walk through the doorway, explore the cabin, approach a seat, and sit. Players can walk around or climb onto a vehicle, remain on the moving roof at safe speed, or be thrown off by excessive speed, acceleration, or aircraft launch. Vehicles collide with players and take projectile and collision damage, including damage from their owner.

Fuel type matters. Fuel is consumed dynamically; it can be siphoned, replenished, contaminated, or used incorrectly. Vehicle statistics and performance reflect damage, fuel, service state, modifications, and refits.

7. Teams

The in-game Team form is dynamic: only actions allowed by membership, role, and delegated permissions are shown. Owners can invite or remove members, appoint managers, delegate invitation/removal/shop permissions, and manage the shared point pool. Kills add points to that pool.

The Team Shop sells team supplies and vehicle kits. Duo Plane, Humvee, APC, and Tank kits each have an independent team-wide five-minute purchase timer. Team members have text chat, a live voice room, and optional team-point translocation to an online teammate.

8. Chat, buffers, and voice

  • / opens global chat.
  • \ opens current-map/mode chat.
  • Shift+\ opens team chat; /t also sends team chat.
  • Alt+\ opens recent private-message contacts and a message box.
  • Alt+O transmits voice using push-to-talk or toggled mode.
  • Alt+Shift+O toggles hearing your own transmitted voice.
  • Alt+V selects positional, channel, private-message, team, or current-map voice rooms.
  • Shift+F7 opens voice settings directly.

Voice settings provide enable/disable, push-to-talk versus toggle, self-monitoring, input and output device selection, isolated microphone testing, and low (16 kbps), standard (24 kbps), or high (48 kbps) quality.

Windows voice commands

Voice commands are separate from live voice chat and are opt-in under Options → Controls. Set a distinctive wake phrase; the default is Infinite Warfare. Say only the wake phrase first. A rising cue and “Listening” confirm that the command window is open. Then say status, health, ammunition, inventory, nearby items, points of interest, next buffer, previous buffer, or repeat message. A closing tone marks the end of capture, and a separate notification tone means the command is being processed. If no command is heard within seven seconds, a closing cue and spoken timeout return the recognizer to wake-phrase mode. Commands cannot fire weapons, use items, transmit chat, or perform destructive actions.

Environmental reverb uses room type, volume, and material. Occlusion traces walls and authored portals separately and can be disabled without disabling reverb.

It uses a near-black background, oversized high-contrast control-type symbols, large focused labels, a stable layout, and compact map and coordinate context. Audio forms update it as focus moves. Enabling it also enables full-screen landscape presentation.

10. Android and touch-screen play

The screen is divided into top, middle, and bottom bands. Gesture actions are rebindable and stored in a gesture JSON file. Gesture practice announces the detected band, gesture, and function when enabled.

  • Dynamic gesture help: bottom-band two-finger triple tap lists only gestures relevant to the current menu, audio form, map, vehicle, or touch keyboard.
  • Menu navigation: one-finger vertical swipes move; double tap activates; two-finger tap goes back; two-finger vertical swipes jump to first/last; two-finger right/left acts as Tab/Shift+Tab in audio forms.
  • Explore by touch: hold one finger nearly still for 450 ms, then drag to discover controls. Movement before the threshold cancels exploration, preventing accidental activation during swipes.
  • Gameplay arbitration: a middle-band drag claims the contact for continuous movement and suppresses the pending aim swipe, reducing unintended aim changes.
  • Touch keyboard: choose the system keyboard, QWERTY explore-by-touch (drag and lift to type), or analogue grid navigation with swipes and double tap.
  • Touch-screen laptops: full-screen bands can expand over the physical display and enable full screen so edge touches cannot activate another window.
  • Orientation: landscape is the default and keeps the top, middle, and bottom bands stacked vertically. Android portrait mode rotates the logical bands into left, centre, and right thirds while preserving every binding assigned to the corresponding band.

Print Screen captures the display to a PNG in the screenshots folder beside the running client. The binding can be changed through the key binding editor.

11. Dynamic help and armoury

The help browser uses audio forms for categories, topics, and read-only content. System documentation remains in text files, while weapons, ammunition, attachments, armour, vehicles, and items are rendered from the live database. Pressing Alt+Enter on inventory content opens the same dynamic read-only help.

The lobby's Dynamic Armoury browses weapons, ammunition, attachments, holsters, suppressors, items, and vehicles; reports compatible content and live statistics; and previews authored sounds through nonblocking play/pause controls without closing or freezing the underlying form.

12. Server, administration, and diagnostics

The dedicated server validates movement, projectiles, hit regions, damage, armour, inventory effects, teams, vehicles, moderation, and reboot/shutdown requests. Packet handling uses bounded draining and batched projectile-audio updates to avoid automatic-fire network floods.

Administration includes account authorization, password-reset requests, player services, warnings and bans, support tickets, bug reports, feedback, server reboot reasons/timers, and confirmed shutdown. Developer-only tools include database grants, give all, POI teleport (Alt+Shift+J), packet reporting (Shift+F10), live packet viewer (Shift+F11), map tools, server manager, and source release packaging.

The source-only release builder compiles the client, optionally creates sounds.dat, copies the executable, sound pack, libraries, changelog, and README into a release directory, then creates a ZIP while reporting step progress and errors.

13. Local files and troubleshooting

  • Preferences, keymaps, keyconfig.json, and gesture configuration are stored under the game's local application-data directory rather than beside the executable. Older files are migrated with a spoken notice.
  • Unhandled exceptions are written to a shareable client error log where NVGT permits recovery. Include that log, the action being performed, map, and approximate time in a bug report.
  • If connection fails, the client resets transient connection state before retrying so a later server recovery does not inherit a half-open session.
  • If positional sound is unclear, check the gameplay/world mixers and independently test reverb and occlusion.
  • If a control is unknown, use Shift+F1; if a touch gesture is unknown, use the context gesture-help gesture.
This README explains systems and default controls. For exact current content statistics, compatibility, cooldowns, doses, ammunition behavior, and vehicle values, use Dynamic Help or the Dynamic Armoury. They read the same database as gameplay.
