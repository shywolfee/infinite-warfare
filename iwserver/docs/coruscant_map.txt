Coruscant Map

Coruscant now links a freight undercity with an elevated civic concourse rather than repeating whole city grids at several heights. Dock Nine dispatch and its bonded cargo store have separate public and loading entrances. Foundry Row leads to a repulsor workshop and parts cage; Condenser Lane serves the coolant control room and reservoir courts.

The civic viaduct and Republic Axis promenade are at elevation 12, with multiple physical stairs and ramps. A clinic, exchange bank, food hall with preparation kitchen, and archive with records vault connect to these elevated routes. Delivery and evacuation paths provide alternatives to the public entrances. The worker's apartment has connected ground-floor rooms and a sleeping room reached by an outside stair.

Furniture has component geometry and can be destroyed. Services are placed in the bank, clinic, waiting areas and workshops. Twelve authored spawn points cover street and concourse locations. Horizontal bounds are 420 by 380.
