# Weapon Handling and Simulation

Every weapon retains an individual handling state in addition to ammunition, attachments, fire mode and ballistics. Alt+N operates the drawn weapon's class-specific control. Alt+Shift+A reports its current handling state. All controls can be changed in the key binding editor.

Pistols have manual safeties. Submachine guns collapse or extend their stocks. Battle rifles switch between mobile and braced shoulder positions. Sniper rifles can be steadied, and bolt-action rifles, such as the AWP, SSG 08, Kar98k and Mosin-Nagant, require their bolts to be cycled after every shot. Light machine guns deploy their bipods only while sitting, and selected heavy machine guns can brace against a wall directly ahead.

Mortars must be deployed before firing. Flamethrowers require their pressure and pilot system to be started. Powered tools such as the chainsaw require their motor to be running. The ballista must be cocked after every launch, while the crossbow retains its variable trigger-held draw-strength simulation.

Short blades, long blades and blunt weapons cycle balanced, power and guard stances. Power attacks cause substantially more authoritative damage but recover slowly and consume more stamina. Guard attacks recover quickly, cost less stamina, cover a wider collision arc and sacrifice damage. Blade length and blunt mass retain their distinct reach, penetration, arc and base recovery behavior.

Weapons do not heat up, foul, wear or jam, and need no servicing. Braces, stocks, bipods and precision holds change actual recoil, server-authoritative dispersion, movement acceleration or recovery rather than serving as descriptive labels.

Revolvers and individually fed shotguns retain their separate manual feed controls: Alt+Y opens or closes the action, Alt+I inserts one round, Alt+Shift+Y cycles a closed tube-fed pump action and Alt+Shift+G ejects ammunition while open. Break actions chamber when closed; detachable-magazine shotguns use the standard reload control.

Weapons in vehicles: you can fire from a seat only with its window open, and melee weapons cannot be used from a vehicle. Firing from a moving vehicle spreads your shots, the more the faster you go, and firing while driving distracts you from the road for a moment. Heavy weapons such as the ballista, field cannon, M2 Browning, NTW-20 and the .50-calibre rifles cannot be fired from inside a vehicle. Rocket launchers hit everyone in the cabin with their backblast, a flamethrower sets the cabin alight, and a mortar round fired inside a vehicle goes off inside it. Mounted weapons are fired with Control from their own seat and are not affected by the vehicle's motion.
