Ammunition System

Every registered weapon declares its calibre, feed device, capacity and standard reserve in the content database. Specialty loads are separate real inventory records and are compatible only with weapons that can logically chamber or consume them.

Press Alt+R to cycle the drawn weapon's compatible loads. Selection affects the next reload and never converts ammunition already loaded. Press A for loaded type, quantity and selected reserve; press R to reload and Shift+R to unload. Alt+A opens the live weapon panel.

Magazine and cell swaps preserve the removed feed device as a typed partial. Revolvers and individually fed shotguns are genuinely manual: Alt+Y opens or closes the cylinder or action, Alt+I inserts exactly one selected round, Alt+Shift+G ejects the contents while open, and Alt+Shift+Y cycles a closed tube-fed pump action. Break actions chamber when closed, while SGX detachable-drum shotguns use magazine swaps. The fire control explains whether an open action, empty chamber, or missed cycle prevents firing. Manual feeds refuse to mix incompatible loads in one cylinder or tube. Extended and drum magazines change real capacity and reserve consumption. Energy weapons preserve cell chemistry and actual remaining charge. Fuel weapons draw only the required fuel from full or partial canisters.

The Help browser's Ammo categories are generated from the current ammunition database. Each entry reports its behavior and every compatible weapon, including shotgun shells, revolver loads, rifle calibres, mortar payloads, crossbow bolts and energy-cell chemistries.

Using a reserve or partial ammunition container explains that ammunition is loaded rather than used directly and lists the compatible weapons you currently carry, including weapons in holsters. Use the drawn weapon's reload controls instead. Selecting a different load with Alt R does not alter rounds already loaded.
