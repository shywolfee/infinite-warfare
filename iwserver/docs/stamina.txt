Stamina System

Stamina is the commitment resource for melee combat and prevents repeated attacks from being spammed.
Every melee stance has a weapon-adjusted stamina cost. An attack is refused before it begins when too little stamina remains.
Stamina restores naturally every 5 seconds.
Press V to report stamina and active effects. Press Alt+B for the full buff and debuff report.
Walking, jumping, heavy equipment and sprinting do not consume stamina or change speed based on stamina. Hold Shift with a movement key for a consistent sprint; weapons classified as too heavy still prevent sprinting.

Press Alt+Z to sit or stand. Sitting greatly slows movement and regenerates melee stamina faster. Heavy weapons cannot be used while sitting, and seated jumps are shorter with a seven-second cooldown.
