Weapon Handling and Simulation

Every weapon retains an individual handling state in addition to ammunition, attachments, fire mode and ballistics. Alt+N operates the drawn weapon's class-specific control. Alt+Shift+N vents, services or cleans its working parts. Alt+Shift+A reports its complete current handling state. All controls can be changed in the key binding editor.

Pistols have manual safeties. Submachine guns collapse or extend their stocks. Assault rifles cycle normal, adverse and suppressed gas settings. Battle rifles switch between mobile and braced shoulder positions. Sniper rifles can be steadied, and the AWP and SSG 08 require their bolts to be cycled after every shot. Light machine guns deploy their bipods only while sitting and accumulate barrel heat during bursts.

Energy weapons accumulate emitter heat separately from cell charge and can enter thermal lockout; cooling mode and manual venting control it. Mortars must be deployed before firing. Flamethrowers require their pressure and pilot system to be started. Powered tools such as the chainsaw require their motor to be running and accumulate drive-system heat. The ballista must be cocked after every launch, while the crossbow retains its variable trigger-held draw-strength simulation.

Short blades, long blades and blunt weapons cycle balanced, power and guard stances. Power attacks cause substantially more authoritative damage but recover slowly and consume more stamina. Guard attacks recover quickly, cost less stamina, cover a wider collision arc and sacrifice damage. Blade length and blunt mass retain their distinct reach, penetration, arc and base recovery behavior.

Firearms and powered systems accumulate fouling or heat as appropriate. Excess heat slows cycling before thermal lockout; excessive fouling prevents operation until field maintenance is performed. Braces, stocks, bipods, precision holds and gas settings change actual recoil, server-authoritative dispersion, movement acceleration or recovery rather than serving as descriptive labels.

Revolvers and shotguns retain their separate manual feed controls: Alt+Y opens or closes the action, Alt+I inserts one round, Alt+Shift+Y cycles the shotgun action and Alt+Shift+G ejects ammunition while open.
