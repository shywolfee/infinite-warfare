Game Modes

Every map in Infinite Warfare is played as a Free for All. In the lobby, open the Modes tab and choose Free for All, then pick which map you want from the map selection menu.

Available maps:

Shattersea: Greater New Aurelia, a four-ward island city crossed by the Crown Canal, five raised bridges, twenty-eight complete interiors, Founders Memorial and Beacon Court.

Coruscant: Four 1280 by 1280 city levels containing 144 named precincts, 72 interiors, 72 raised landmarks, 20 major roads and a shared grand turbolift.

Ghost Town: A sprawling deserted city whose streets, neighborhoods, commons, construction tracts, woods, ridges and outskirts all have distinct terrain and navigation names.

Freya's Ascent: Five enormous rising settlements with twenty-five enclosed buildings, dual ascent routes and a five-chamber subterranean Rootvault network.

Select a map and you will be deployed directly to it with a fresh loadout.
