Rewards

Kill rewards are decided by the server and count kills within one life. Three kills: five fragmentation grenades. Five kills: a bottle of Trauma Repair Serum and 40 credits. Ten kills: 75 credits. Each comes once per life: dying and redeploying from the lobby starts the count again, and being revived by a teammate does not.

Every kill also earns 10 to 20 credits, which count towards your team's points as well. Killing the same player more than ten times within fifteen minutes earns nothing further.
