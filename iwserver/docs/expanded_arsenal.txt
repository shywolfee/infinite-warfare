# Expanded arsenal

## Tactical firearms

The new tactical firearms use their own recorded shots and receiver handling.
Every firearm has a separately labelled ammunition supply in the supply store.
Calibres include .357 SIG, .50 Action Express, 5.7x28mm, .338 Lapua Magnum,
9x19mm, .45 ACP, rifle ammunition and 12-gauge shells. Twin pistols carry their
combined magazine capacity; shotgun tubes load individual shells.

## Galactic blasters

Blasters consume energy cartridges and also build heat. Heat and ammunition
are separate: a loaded cartridge cannot make an overheated receiver fire.
Heavy and precision blasters heat more per shot than repeaters.

Use the weapon-operation control to vent. Venting temporarily prevents firing;
passive cooling also works while the weapon is put away. Weapon status reports
heat percentage, venting and overheating. After overheating, the receiver must
cool to a quarter of its heat capacity before it can fire again.

Ion disruptors slow opponents and temporarily disable vehicle electronics.
Incinerator blasters ignite targets. Bow and cycler weapons use physical
ammunition rather than Tibanna cartridges.

## Mercenary specialists

Medical projectors and rescue crossbows heal teammates, stop bleeding and
extinguish fire. They do not heal enemies, arena opponents or spectators, and
cannot raise health above 1,000. Aim at your teammate; firing still consumes
ammunition. These are aimed projectiles, not automatic target-lock beams.

Repair crossbows and the Foundry wrench repair allied or unoccupied vehicle
hulls. They cannot resurrect wrecks and do not injure players. Flame weapons
and flare launchers ignite direct-hit targets. Injector weapons can slow
targets or return a proportion of actual damage as health to their shooter.

Springboard and Vaultspring training launchers are non-damaging practice
weapons. They are not movement-boost equipment. Demolition launchers use
impact explosions, not remotely detonated sticky charges.

## Content and sound installation

All weapons, ammunition and shop listings are editable content files. Thermal
rates and specialist effects are weapon properties; they are not inferred
from weapon names. Deploy the updated client and server with the rebuilt
sounds.dat archive. The import tool needs the owner's local sound libraries;
source recordings are not stored in Git.

Confusing older receiver variants have new filenames and inventory IDs.
Old inventory IDs are deliberately not retained or migrated. Replace those
saved inventories instead of expecting aliases to old weapons.
