New in 0.5.0.5, build 78 (2026-08-29)
Fixed Hear your own projectile flight loops and near-miss sounds not producing a flyby for shots fired into open space. The server now evaluates the shooter as a valid flyby listener after the projectile clears the muzzle, sends exactly one owner-tagged positional cue for client-side option filtering, and never applies machine-gun suppression penalties to the shooter's own projectile.
Fixed Compress a release build failing with `Bad path syntax` when the project directory or `Infinite Warfare.nvgt` contains spaces. Compiler, sound-pack, source-restart and PowerShell archive launches now use NVGT 0.90's structured process argument arrays, preserve every path as one argument, use the project as their working directory, and validate the actual child-process exit code.
Added sixteen construction-specific armour patching supplies for light fabric, Kevlar, ballistic, plate and heavy armour across head, torso, arm and leg slots. Patching is server-authoritative, consumes the patch, restores only seven percent of original durability and can never raise armour beyond sixty percent, so it extends field life without replacing real armour.
Fixed Developer Give All treating dynamic vehicle catalogue records as inventory items. Vehicle records remain available to Armoury and Help but are now explicitly excluded from inventory grants.
Redesigned the dynamic Armoury category loader to query the unified content database for every category instead of special-casing only weapons and bypassing item records. Ammo, explosives, healing, armour, armour repair, weapon maintenance, vehicle supplies, attachments, holsters, team equipment, tools, electronics, containers, ordinary equipment and vehicles now populate independently.
Expanded inventory organization with dedicated Armour Repair, Weapon Maintenance, Vehicle Supplies, Holsters, Tools, Electronics and Containers categories, replacing the increasingly overloaded generic Utility grouping.
Added Alt+Shift+V as a configurable nearby Vehicle Workshop. Its live NV_form reports health, armour, fuel quantity and required type, engine, transmission, tyre and cooling condition, contamination, effective speed and every installed modification, then exposes only deliberate service/refit operations.
Added fuel simulation to every vehicle. Jeeps, pickups, buggies and sedans use gasoline; Humvees, APCs, tanks and vans use diesel; the Duo Plane uses aviation fuel. Each type has a logical tank capacity and consumption rate modified by condition, contamination, performance tuning, turbocharging and auxiliary armour; empty tanks and failed engines or transmissions stop movement.
Added working fuel transfer and wrong-fuel consequences. Players can refuel from typed twenty-litre cans, siphon ten litres with a hose and empty approved can, accidentally contaminate and damage an engine with incompatible fuel, or drain and partially flush the system with workshop supplies.
Added persistent per-instance engine, transmission, tyre and cooling condition with gradual driving wear, reduced performance, increased consumption, contamination-triggered damage and component failure. Timed oil/filter, transmission, tyre, cooling and fuel-system services require their logical reusable tools and consume real supplies.
Added six functional vehicle refits: performance ECU, off-road tyre set, reinforced suspension, auxiliary armour, turbocharger and extended fuel tank. Refits consume their parts and alter effective speed, fuel demand, protection, mass trade-offs or capacity rather than acting as cosmetic labels.
Added 35 vehicle fuel, service, diagnostic, repair and customization items, including typed fuels, siphoning equipment, filters and fluids, jack and tyre supplies, battery and ignition parts, brake and cooling parts, workshop tools, electrical parts and all refit packages. Bags and Boxes now distribute randomized armour-patching and vehicle-workshop bundles during normal play.
Added weapon mechanical condition, alignment, jams and permanent damage. Poor condition can cause real malfunctions, slows the action, increases recoil and dispersion, and lowers server-authoritative projectile damage, velocity and range; the live weapon panel reports the resulting statistics and exact condition state.
Added unsafe improvised weapon maintenance. When correct supplies are missing, an Improvised Weapon Tool permits an explicitly confirmed attempt that can damage condition and alignment, create permanent damage or completely break the weapon. Jams require a clearing rod or gunsmith multitool, while timed repairs require replacement parts and alignment gauges and cannot erase permanent structural damage; sufficiently ruined receivers are irreparable.
Added fourteen gunsmith repair, diagnostic, replacement and emergency items for damaged actions, fire-control parts, extraction, springs, headspace, receiver reinforcement, energy contacts, optic alignment and malfunction clearing.
Connected authored audio to the new systems without doubled playback: armour patching uses the dedicated armour repair cue, liquid filling and siphoning use their respective transfer sounds, general servicing uses the Nanomatic tool cue, and installed vehicle refits use a mechanical attachment cue.
Replaced the unreliable four-finger double tap with the previously unused bottom-band two-finger triple tap for context-sensitive gesture help. Updated the global recognizer, touch keyboard, gesture practice, contextual help, and complete Android gesture reference; no four-finger recognition is required for this action anymore.
Redesigned weapon maintenance as a timed, inventory-backed simulation. Servicing now immobilizes the player for eight to eighteen seconds while networking remains live, announces meaningful countdown checkpoints, imposes a 45-second per-weapon service cooldown, consumes appropriate materials, and only partially clears heat or fouling instead of instantly resetting it.
Added 31 dynamic maintenance items covering reusable handgun, rifle, shotgun, machine-gun, energy-emitter, fuel-system, powered-drive, edged and blunt-weapon tools plus consumable patches, solvents, brushes, lubricants, coolants, vent cartridges, hot barrels, pressure seals, chain supplies, blade treatments, grip wraps and retaining compound. Every item is registered in the content database, Armoury, categorized inventory, dynamic Help and developer give-all/item picker.
Added class-specific maintenance requirements and limitations. Energy weapons require an emitter key, vent cartridge and dielectric coolant; hot machine guns require a barrel wrench, protective gloves and spare barrel; flamethrowers require fuel-system tools, nozzle cleaner and seals; powered tools require drive tools, lubricant and coolant; firearm classes use their logical cleaning equipment; and melee servicing now requires edge or impact-specific supplies.
Added nine complete maintenance bundle families to ordinary loot containers. Bags now provide one randomized class-appropriate tool-and-supply bundle and Boxes provide two, making every required maintenance item obtainable through regular play rather than only developer grants.
Fixed Compress a release build reporting failure after PowerShell had successfully produced release.zip. Completion now depends on the newly created archive rather than NVGT's non-exit-code process return value.
Fixed release executables failing at startup with "Failed to determine API version used for plugin nvgt_curl." The builder now replaces any stale project copy with the API-compatible nvgt_curl.dll from the exact NVGT installation compiling the executable before compression.
Added persistent, fully rebindable Android gesture mappings under Options, Controls. Every discrete gameplay gesture is resolved through the local application-data iw/gesture_config.json file, conflicting assignments are swapped safely, and the same accessible editor can restore the complete default layout.
Fixed the Changelog viewer only exposing its first line by presenting the complete changelog as a read-only multiline NV_form input.

Fixed unreliable and glitchy four-finger gestures. Four fingers are now aggregated by a dedicated full-screen pass-through recognizer instead of being divided among whichever top, middle, and bottom bands the individual fingers happened to touch; dynamic gesture help and gesture practice consume that single global event and clear residual band events before returning.
Added the persistent Expand all touch bands across the full laptop screen option under Options, Display. On a non-mobile touch-screen computer it enables touch input simulation and native full screen together, allowing the existing top, middle, and bottom normalized bands to cover the complete physical display rather than only a window.
Added menu and audio-form explore by touch, enabled by default and independently toggleable under Options, Display. Holding one finger within a small stationary tolerance for 450 milliseconds arms exploration; only then does dragging map the finger's vertical screen position to controls or menu items and announce changes. Immediate swipes, taps, double taps, and fingers that move before the hold threshold explicitly cancel the exploration candidate, preventing accidental announcements.
Fixed incomplete audio-form gesture navigation. Two-finger swipe right now performs Tab and two-finger swipe left performs Shift+Tab, including correctly entering the selected options category page, moving through its controls, returning to the category strip at the first control, and leaving the page after its last control. The context-sensitive gesture help and full Android gesture reference document these controls.
Added four-finger gesture recognition and a dynamic four-finger-double-tap help action. It opens a touch-navigable reference filtered to the current context, with distinct instructions for audio forms, menus, map gameplay, vehicles, and either touch-keyboard mode; gesture practice and the complete Android reference recognize the new action as well.
Added an optional in-game touch keyboard for editable NV_form fields while preserving the system keyboard as the default. Explore-by-touch mode maps the full screen to a five-row QWERTY layout, announces keys while a finger travels across them, and types the last explored key on release. Analogue mode presents the same keyboard as a navigable grid: one-finger swipes move by key or row, double tap types, and two-finger directional swipes jump to top-middle, bottom-middle, middle-left, or middle-right. Both modes provide one-shot Shift, persistent Caps Lock, digits, punctuation, Backspace, Space, Done, live networking during entry, and separate persistent toggles in Options, Display.

It can be toggled from the new Display category in Options or at any time with Ctrl+Shift+F12; failed mode changes retain the previous preference, and ordinary Windows gameplay mechanics are unchanged.
Added Android-only gestures for the recently introduced actions that lacked touch access, using previously unassigned band, finger-count and hold combinations. The new mappings cover ammunition selection, item configuration and inspection, quickbar management, weapon operation, maintenance and inspection, every manual-fed weapon operation, body-region targeting and reset, sitting and standing, jumping, global/map/team/private chat access, voice transmission/rooms/settings/self-monitoring, effects, and equipment. Desktop and Windows key handling and mechanics are unchanged.

Android touch gestures
Added full touchscreen control for Android. When the game runs on a mobile device it now reads touch gestures and turns them into game actions, so the whole game can be played without a keyboard. The screen is split into three horizontal bands: the top band handles inventory and status, the middle band handles movement and combat, and the bottom band handles the message buffers and the game menu. Gestures are recognised by which band your finger starts in and how many fingers you use: for example a one finger swipe left or right in the top band cycles your inventory, a two finger tap in the middle band fires your weapon, and a two finger double tap in the bottom band opens the game menu. Driving a vehicle unlocks extra middle-band gestures such as a three finger swipe to change seats and a one finger drag up or down to accelerate. A full reference is available from a new "view Android touch gestures" entry in the main menu and from the options menu; you can also reach it from the in-game menu on a phone.
Because Android does not support the desktop audio form windows, the menus and dialogs those windows provided are replaced on Android with touch-navigable menus and the native on-screen keyboard, so account entry, the game menu, equipment, the player and tracking lists, the weapon panel, help entries, and points of interest all work on a phone. Menus can be driven by swiping up or down to move, double tapping to choose, and a two finger tap to go back. Nothing changes on desktop. An option, "simulate Android touch mode", is available in the options menu for testing the touch interface on a computer.

  Press Alt+S to open the Support menu during gameplay. The menu contains:
    Open a new ticket: enter a subject and description. Admins and developers logged into the server are notified immediately when a new ticket is submitted.
    View my open tickets: browse your currently open tickets. Select a ticket to view its full details and any staff replies.
    View my closed tickets: browse your archived/closed tickets and their close reasons.
  Ticket viewing shows the ticket ID, submitter, status, description, and all replies in order.
  Players and staff can reply to open tickets, creating a back-and-forth conversation thread.
  Players can close their own tickets without providing a reason.
  Admins and developers must provide a reason when closing a ticket. The close reason is displayed on the ticket.
  Admins and developers see an additional "View all open tickets (Staff)" option in the Support menu, listing every open ticket from all players.
  Developers can also manage tickets from the Administrator Panel in the main menu without logging into a character. The admin panel now includes a "Manage support tickets" button that lists all open tickets and allows viewing, replying, and closing with a required reason.

New in 0.4.9.5, build 25 (2026-06-01)
Added holster system. Equip up to two holsters in the new Holster 1 and Holster 2 equipment slots. Different holster types are designed for different weapon classes:
  Pistol Holster: holds Glock 17, Beretta 92FS, or Desert Eagle.
  .357 Gun Belt: holds the Taurus Model 66 revolver. Stores up to 24 rounds of .357 Magnum ammunition in the belt.
  .44 Gun Belt: holds the Magnum Revolver. Stores up to 24 rounds of .44 Magnum ammunition in the belt.
  SMG Sling: holds MP5, UZI, MP7, or P90.
  Rifle Strap: holds HK G36, AK-47, TAR-21, FAMAS, M4A1, SCAR-H, or FN FAL.
  Sniper Carry Case: holds SG-08, AWP, or Dragunov SVD.
  Shotgun Bandoleer: holds Benelli M4, KS-123, or SPAS-12. Stores up to 16 shells of 12-gauge buckshot.
  Knife Sheath: holds the combat knife.
  Sword Scabbard: holds the machete or katana.
  Bat Holster: holds the baseball bat.
Press Shift+Backspace to holster your currently drawn weapon into a compatible equipped holster. If multiple holsters can hold the weapon, you choose which one.
Press Shift+Backspace again with no weapon drawn to draw a holstered weapon. If both holsters hold weapons, a menu shows each holster name, weapon name, and loaded ammunition count in parentheses.
Gun belts and the shotgun bandoleer can store ammunition. Use an ammo belt or shell box item from your inventory while a compatible belt is equipped to load rounds into the belt. When drawing a weapon from a gun belt with stored ammo, the weapon is automatically reloaded to capacity from the belt.
Stored ammunition is displayed in the holster's equipment menu listing and inventory display.
Press Alt+E to open the equipment menu from gameplay.
Press Alt+Backspace to unequip a holster. If the holster contains a weapon, it is returned to your inventory. If the belt contains ammunition, it is returned as well.

New in 0.457, build 11 (2026-03-20)

  Modes: lists available game modes. Currently Battlegrounds is available.
    Select Battlegrounds and press Enter to deploy to the main map and begin playing.
  Account Info: displays your username, email, registration status, and duo mate.
  Players: queries the server for the live in-game player list and reads it aloud.
  Rewards: describes the kill-reward milestones (3 kills and 5 kills).
Leaving the lobby returns you to the main menu without entering the game world.
The server now handles the lobby_players request from pre-spawn clients.

New in 0.18
Weapons now use a realistic magazine and clip-based ammo system.
Each weapon has a specific feed device with a real-world capacity and must be reloaded manually.
Press R to reload. The current feed device is ejected and a fresh one is loaded from reserve.
Press Shift+R to unload the current magazine back into your reserve pool.
Press A to hear rounds loaded, ammo type, feed device name, and rounds in reserve.
When a weapon runs dry, you hear the empty click and a reminder to press R.
Firing is blocked while a reload is in progress.
Beta: all players start with a full weapon and generous reserve magazines.
Ammo types and feed devices by weapon:
  Glock 17: 9x19mm Parabellum, 17-round magazine (10 spare)
  Beretta 92FS: 9x19mm Parabellum, 15-round magazine (10 spare)
  Taurus Model 66: .357 Magnum, 6-round cylinder (12 spare)
  Magnum Revolver: .44 Magnum, 6-round cylinder (12 spare)
  FN P90: FN 5.7x28mm, 50-round magazine (6 spare)
  HK G36: 5.56x45mm NATO, 30-round magazine (8 spare)
  AK-47: 7.62x39mm, 30-round magazine (8 spare)
  TAR-21: 5.56x45mm NATO, 30-round magazine (8 spare)
  FAMAS F1: 5.56x45mm NATO, 25-round magazine (8 spare)
  Benelli M4: 12-gauge buckshot, 8-round tube magazine (8 spare)
  KS-123: 12-gauge buckshot, 5-round tube magazine (8 spare)
  M1 Garand: .30-06 Springfield, 8-round en-bloc clip (8 spare)
  SG-08 Sniper: .308 Winchester, 10-round magazine (5 spare)
  120mm Mortar: 120mm HE mortar shell, single-load barrel (5 spare)

New in 0.17
Added "View the changelog" option to the main menu so you can read patch notes without being in-game.
Added an in-game game menu, opened with the grave accent key (the key above Tab, left of 1).
The game menu contains four options: View changes, View rules, View account info, and View developers.
Account info shows your username, duo mate, and registered email address.
The developers screen lists the full Firegaming development team.

New in 0.16
The server must now be reachable before you can register a new account or log in via the options menu.
If the server is offline, both actions are blocked with a clear error message.
Added server_online() network probe that checks connectivity before sensitive account operations.

New in 0.15
Added a full account system.
Accounts are stored in iwserver/accounts/ alongside the server data.
Register a new account from the main menu: choose a username, password, confirm the password, and provide a valid email address.
You must read and agree to the server rules before your account is created.
The options menu now has a "Set account (login)" option which replaces the old "Set your name" entry.
Enter your registered username and password to log in and set your in-game name.
Passwords are stored encrypted with a per-account salt.
