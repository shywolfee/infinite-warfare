Ghost Town Map

Mercy Street and Assay Road form the spine of a newly built abandoned town. The Last Lantern saloon contains a public bar room, kitchen, rear passage and upstairs guest/card rooms reached by an exterior gallery stair. The assay bank has a customer room and a separate weighing office.

The sheriff's office connects to a detention passage and two cells. Across town are the doctor's consulting room and dispensary, followed by the farrier's forge. The western railway depot has ticket and parcels rooms on a raised embankment, with a goods ramp and another exit.

Outside routes include the dry wash, orchard and raised cemetery knoll. An incline leads to the chapel and grave markers. Destructible furniture provides cover; service fixtures retain stock and cooldown limits. Twelve safe spawn points are distributed across the map. Horizontal bounds are 400 by 360.
